McKinsey Argentina

We work with leaders in every sector of Argentina’s economy to inspire innovation and accelerate sustainable and inclusive growth.
McKinsey is Argentina’s leading management-consulting firm. With more than 30 years of presence in Buenos Aires, our partner group is fully local, and our team embraces more than 100 top professionals from Argentina and across the world. We work with major companies in every sector of the economy, as well as with entrepreneurial start-ups and senior leaders in the public sector. Everything we do is focused on helping our clients achieve greater impact: shaping bold growth strategies, harnessing the power of digital, transforming operations, and bringing out the best in their people.
